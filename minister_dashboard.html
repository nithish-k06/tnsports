1. New Stadium Construction Process

End-to-end greenfield facility development lifecycle

Greenfield Build
1
Site Selection & Land Clearance Identify district plot, verify soil quality & local venue accessibility.
2
Cabinet Sanction & State Budget Allocation Minister issues G.O. for capital funding & seating capacity targets.
3
Civil Tendering & Structural Construction Award contract for pavilion, seating tier, indoor courts & floodlight towers.
4
Handover & Public Sports Dedication Final inspection, sports association clearance & operational inauguration.

2. Existing Stadium Upgrade Process

Modernization of legacy district grounds & complexes

Facility Renovation
1
Facility Condition Audit & Upgrade Scope Inspect track wear, lighting lux level, turf condition & court seating.
2
Executive Directive & DSO Authorization Issue Minister G.O. directing DSO to initiate targeted facility upgrade.
3
Specialized Installation (Track / Lights / Turf) Install IAAF Class-1 synthetic tracks, 1200 Lux LEDs or wooden courts.
4
Certification Sign-off & Re-opening Audit sports standards compliance and resume athlete training.
