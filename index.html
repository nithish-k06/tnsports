Our Vision

To position Tamil Nadu as a premier sports state in India by establishing world-class infrastructure, nurturing grassroots athletic talent, and cultivating a sustainable culture of sportsmanship and competitive excellence.

Our Mission

Empower athletes, coaches, and sports officers across all 38 districts of Tamil Nadu through structured financial assistance, state-of-the-art training facilities, transparent trial management, and comprehensive digital governance.

  • Structured financial stipends & grant support for grassroots athletes.
  • High-performance coaching certification across 38 districts.
  • Transparent trial management & digital event scheduling.
